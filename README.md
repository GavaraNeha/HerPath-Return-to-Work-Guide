## 💡 The Problem

Women returning to work after a career break — whether for caregiving, family responsibilities, or other life circumstances — often struggle to find guidance that accounts for their real-world constraints: limited hours, tight budgets, and skills that feel out of date. Most career tools also fail to reframe a career break as **experience**, rather than a gap to apologize for.

## ✨ The Solution

**HerPath** is a personalized AI career-return engine. It asks the user about her career goals, skills, real-life constraints, and preferences — then generates a complete, realistic **Career Dashboard**, including:

- 🎯 Career options with fit scores
- 🧩 Transferable skills identified from past experience
- 📅 A 30/60/90-day action plan
- 🗓️ A weekly schedule
- 📚 Learning resource recommendations
- 💼 A suggested portfolio project
- 🎤 Interview preparation tips
- 🔄 A recovery plan
- 📊 A readiness score
- ➡️ One clear "next best action"

---

## 🧠 How It Works

Built entirely on **AWS PartyRock**, powered by **Claude Sonnet** as the underlying LLM.

### Input Sections
| Section | What It Captures |
|---|---|
| 💼 **01 — Career** | Career goal, education background, previous work experience |
| 🛠️ **02 — Skills** | Technical skills, soft skills |
| ⏰ **03 — Real-Life Constraints** | Career break details, available hours/week, learning budget, responsibilities |
| 🎯 **04 — Preferences** | Preferred work arrangement, confidence level, accessibility/learning preferences |

### Output
A single AI-generated **Career Dashboard** widget synthesizes all inputs into an actionable, personalized roadmap covering all 11 output sections listed above.
